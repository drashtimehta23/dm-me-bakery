Our Story

Two bakers. One belief.

Dm.Me.Bakery started the way most good things do — in a home kitchen, with a mixing bowl, a shared sweet tooth, and a challenge: could eggless and vegan baking still be rich, soft, and craveable? A few orders later, our home kitchen became the go-to bakery for birthdays, baby showers, and everyday celebrations.

No eggs. No dairy.
No compromise on flavor.
What We Bake

Eggless or Dairy Free Baked Goods!

Below is a menu of our baked goods — any of them can be fully custom pieces for your celebrations that call for something extra.

Cakes

Custom eggless cakes made for your special moments! From elegant everyday cakes to statement tiered cakes and adorable smash cakes, personalized with your choice of flavors, colors, and design.

Cupcakes

Soft, fluffy eggless cupcakes baked fresh and decorated to match your celebration. Perfect for parties, gifting, and sweet little treats.

Cake Cups

Layers of moist cake, creamy frosting, and delicious fillings served in individual cups. It is a fun and mess-free way to enjoy your favorite cake flavors.

Brownies

Rich, fudgy brownies with the perfect chewy center and crinkle top! Baked fresh and made for sharing, gifting, or treating yourself.

Bread and Bagels

Freshly baked, soft and flavorful breads that are perfect for everyday enjoyment, gifting, or adding something homemade to your table.

Custom Orders

Thoughtfully curated custom treats and cake hampers designed for your special occasions — featuring cakes, flowers, balloons, chocolates, and more, all beautifully arranged and personalized for your celebration.

Policies & Guidelines

Important Order Information

To ensure your custom order is perfect, please review our key policies below.

Order Ahead

Please place cake orders at least 4–5 days in advance to ensure availability and thoughtful preparation.

Same Day Orders

Same day orders for cakes have a $20 surcharge. For cupcakes, the surcharge is $10. We do not accept same day orders for breads.

Modifications

Please notify us of any order changes at least 24 hours in advance. Design-specific changes may be subject to an additional charge.
